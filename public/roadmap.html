From this prototype to the finished product

You have just seen the full patient journey working end to end on a local server. This page explains what each step does today, and what we add to make it safe for real patients.

The end result

A patient describes symptoms in their own language, meets an available specialist on encrypted video with live subtitles, pays once the call ends, and instantly receives a signed prescription and a numbered invoice. The doctor sees requests live and writes the prescription during the call. Every step is logged, secured, and works on a phone or a laptop.

Honest limits of this prototype

Payment is simulated and the signature is ours, so the prescription is marked as a prototype and is not a valid medical document. Chat keys disappear if the browser tab is reloaded. Live captions use the browser's speech recognition, which sends audio to the browser maker, so they are not end-to-end private. The final version addresses each of these.
